## Deliverables

### 🛳 Week 1–2 (Titanic Dataset)
- **[Data Cleaning](ca://s?q=Titanic_data_cleaning_steps)**: handled missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent entries.  
- **[Feature Engineering](ca://s?q=Titanic_feature_engineering)**: created FamilySize and extracted Title from names.  
- **[Exploratory Analysis](ca://s?q=Titanic_exploratory_data_analysis)**: univariate and bivariate analysis with visualizations.  
- **[Insights](ca://s?q=Titanic_dataset_insights)**: survival strongly influenced by gender, class, fare, and age.

### 📊 Week 3 (Statistics & Probability)
- **[Descriptive Statistics](ca://s?q=Descriptive_statistics_in_Titanic_dataset)**: mean, variance, and standard deviation for Age and Fare.  
- **[Probability Distributions](ca://s?q=Probability_distributions_in_Titanic_dataset)**: Age ~ normal distribution; Fare ~ skewed distribution.  
- **[Hypothesis Testing](ca://s?q=Hypothesis_testing_in_Titanic_dataset)**: Chi-square test confirmed survival differences between genders.  
- **[Correlation vs Causation](ca://s?q=Correlation_vs_causation_in_Titanic_dataset)**: Fare and Pclass correlated with survival, but causation linked to socioeconomic status.  


## Key Insights
- Women and children had higher survival rates.  
- Passenger class and fare strongly influenced survival outcomes.  
- Statistical tests confirmed gender differences in survival were significant.  
- Random Forest identified **Sex, Pclass, Fare, Title** as the most important predictors.  
- Reinforced the importance of **statistical thinking** in guiding predictive modeling.
